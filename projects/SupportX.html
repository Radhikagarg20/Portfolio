About Project

SupportX.ai is a full-stack AI-powered customer support platform designed to streamline customer service through intelligent automation. The platform centralizes customer interactions, manages support tickets, provides AI-assisted responses, and delivers real-time analytics through an interactive dashboard. Built with a scalable architecture, SupportX.ai enhances agent productivity while improving customer satisfaction with faster and smarter support experiences.

Problem Statement

Modern businesses often struggle with fragmented customer communication, delayed response times, manual ticket handling, and limited visibility into support performance. Traditional support systems lack intelligent automation, making it difficult to deliver fast, personalized, and scalable customer service.

Solution

SupportX.ai provides a centralized AI-driven customer support platform that combines intelligent chat assistance, automated ticket management, secure authentication, real-time analytics, and role-based dashboards. The platform enables support teams to resolve customer issues faster, reduce repetitive tasks, and deliver a seamless support experience.
