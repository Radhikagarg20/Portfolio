About Project

FinIntel AI is a production-ready full-stack financial intelligence platform that analyzes publicly listed companies using a custom machine learning-based financial scoring engine. The platform fetches company financial data, calculates key financial metrics, classifies businesses into investment risk categories, and presents actionable insights through an interactive investor dashboard with dynamic visualizations.

Problem Statement

Retail investors often struggle to evaluate company fundamentals due to complex financial statements and scattered financial information. Existing platforms provide raw numbers but rarely offer an easy-to- understand financial score or automated insights that simplify investment decisions.

Solution

FinIntel AI automatically retrieves company financial data, builds meaningful financial metrics, applies a custom ML-based scoring algorithm, categorizes companies as Strong, Stable, or Risky, and generates strengths, weaknesses, and visual analytics through a responsive investor dashboard.
